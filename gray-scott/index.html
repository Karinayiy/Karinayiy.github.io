Reaction–diffusion patterns on the plane and on curved surfaces · ← Boyan (Karina) Li

∂u/∂t = Dᵤ∇²u − uv² + F(1 − u),   ∂v/∂t = Dᵥ∇²v + uv² − (F + k)v

01What you are seeing

Two chemicals diffuse and react. u is fed in at rate F; v turns u into more of itself (u + 2v → 3v) and is removed at rate F + k. Bright regions are high v.

02Why patterns form

Diffusion usually smooths things out. Turing (1952) showed that when two species diffuse at different speeds, diffusion can instead destabilise a uniform state and select a preferred wavelength. The right-hand panels compute exactly where that happens.

03On curved surfaces

On a surface the Laplacian becomes the Laplace–Beltrami operator, discretised here with the cotangent formula. Compare where spots settle on the inside and outside of a torus, where the Gaussian curvature changes sign.
